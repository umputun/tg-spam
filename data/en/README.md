# English samples

`ham-samples.txt` and `spam-samples.txt` combine samples written for this set with messages of 50 characters or more selected at random from the [SMS Spam Collection](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5CC84) by Tiago Almeida and José María Gómez Hidalgo (2011), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). In the SMS messages, HTML entities are decoded, the `<#>` placeholder is replaced with a number, and duplicates are removed.

`exclude-tokens.txt` and `stop-words.txt` are copies of the preset files in `data`.
